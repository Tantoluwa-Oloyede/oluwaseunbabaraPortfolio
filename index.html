The person behind the pixels

Technology is the tool.
Feeling is the point.

My journey into AI video began with curiosity—and a practical question: how could these tools create something useful for businesses and brands?

I invested in training, learned from experienced creators and experimented with new tools. I wanted to go beyond making beautiful images and videos to understand how to put the technology to work.

As my skills grew, I began creating videos for brands, exploring creative concepts and taking on client projects. I now also teach others how to create with AI and apply the skill in practical ways.

AI keeps evolving, so I keep learning, refining my craft and finding new ways to bring ideas to life.
